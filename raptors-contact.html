Avian Habitat & Falconry

Birds of Prey Sanctuary & Conservation

The rocky cliffs, old-growth timber, and open meadows of our 1,917-acre Chama Valley property provide critical territory for golden eagles, red-tailed hawks, peregrine falcons, and native owls.

  • Visiting Educational Falconry: Hosting licensed visiting falconers for safe, strictly regulated educational demonstrations and avian appreciation.
  • Protected Hunting Grounds: Strict zero-predator-hunting and chemical-free land policies preserve natural prey populations.
  • Educational Outreach: Introducing veterans and youth mentees to raptor ecology and ethical wildlife stewardship.
