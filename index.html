Welcome to the Sanctuary

Located in the high-country meadows and timberlands of Chama, New Mexico, The D.G. Chama Sanctuary Project is a dedicated conservation and wildlife management initiative spanning 1,917 acres in the heart of the Chama Valley.

Founded by David J. O’Bryon, our sanctuary is built on a simple, enduring commitment: to protect native wildlife habitats, maintain pristine catch-and-release trout waters, and provide peaceful, low-cost outdoor opportunities for disabled veterans and single-parent families.

Our Three Core Pillars

1. Wildlife & Habitat Conservation

We actively steward 1,917 acres of high-altitude rangeland, forest, and riparian corridors to support thriving populations of native Rocky Mountain elk, mule deer, wild turkey, and upland game birds. Through dedicated food plots, strict non-motorized ethical standards, and a strict no-predator-hunting policy, we ensure the land remains a true refuge for native wildlife.

2. Pristine Catch-and-Release Trout Waters

Our solar-fed high-country ponds and cold-water streams are managed strictly as 100% catch-and-release fisheries. By protecting riparian habitats and promoting responsible angling ethics, we preserve native Rio Grande Cutthroat trout ecosystems for future generations of conservationists.

3. Disabled Veterans & Youth Outdoor Access

We believe the healing quiet of the outdoors should be accessible to those who sacrificed for our country and to single-parent families. In partnership with military, veteran, and community organizations, the sanctuary provides hosted, low-cost wildlife management hunts, adaptive fly-fishing retreats, and youth conservation mentorships.

Birds of Prey & Raptor Conservation

Beyond big game and cold-water fisheries, The D.G. Chama Sanctuary Project supports native birds of prey through wild rangeland hunting perches, nesting boxes, fence flight diverters, and visiting educational falconry demonstrations—inspiring the next generation to respect and protect New Mexico’s wild heritage.
