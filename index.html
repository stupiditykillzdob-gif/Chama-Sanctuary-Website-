Preserving Northern New Mexico’s Wildlife. Serving Those Who Served.

Located in the high-country meadows and timberlands of Chama, New Mexico, The D.G. Chama Sanctuary Project is a dedicated conservation and wildlife management initiative spanning 1,917 acres in the heart of the Chama Valley.

Welcome to the Sanctuary

Founded by David J. O’Bryon, our sanctuary is built on a simple, enduring commitment: to protect native wildlife habitats, maintain pristine catch-and-release trout waters, and provide peaceful, low-cost outdoor opportunities for disabled veterans and single-parent families.

Our Three Core Pillars

1. Wildlife & Habitat Conservation

We actively steward 1,917 acres of high-altitude rangeland, forest, and riparian corridors to support thriving populations of native Rocky Mountain elk, mule deer, wild turkey, and upland game birds through dedicated food plots, strict non-motorized walk-in ethics, and a zero-predator-hunting policy.

2. Pristine Catch-and-Release Trout Waters

Our solar-fed high-country ponds and cold-water streams are managed strictly as 100% catch-and-release fisheries using barbless, single-hook, lead-free tungsten-beaded nymphs to preserve native Rio Grande Cutthroat trout ecosystems for future generations.

3. Disabled Veterans & Youth Access

We believe the healing quiet of the outdoors should be accessible to those who sacrificed for our country and to single-parent families. We provide hosted, low-cost wildlife management hunts, three-tier adaptive fly-fishing retreats, and youth conservation mentorships.

Birds of Prey & Raptor Conservation

Beyond big game and cold-water fisheries, The D.G. Chama Sanctuary Project supports native birds of prey through wild rangeland hunting perches, nesting boxes, fence flight diverters, zero chemical rodenticides, and visiting educational falconry demonstrations.
